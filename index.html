ECHO DEBT

Every action has a cost. Every past has an echo.

How To Play

  • Move with arrows / WASD / on-screen buttons.
  • Every move you make is recorded.
  • Press RETRY to restart the level — your last attempt becomes a transparent Echo that repeats its moves automatically.
  • Stand your Echo on a switch (◆) to open a door (▦) so your current self can pass.
  • Reach the glowing exit (◉) to complete the level.
